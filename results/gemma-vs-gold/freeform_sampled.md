## I have chicken, rice, and broccoli. Give me a simple dinner recipe using them.

```
Okay, here's a simple and delicious chicken, rice, and broccoli dinner recipe:

**Chicken & Rice Stir-Fry**

This recipe is quick, easy, and packed with flavor.

**Ingredients:**

*   1.5 lbs Chicken Breast, diced
*   1 cup Cooked Rice
*   1/2 cup Chicken Broth (low sodium preferred)
*   1 cup Broccoli florets, chopped
*   2 tbsp Soy Sauce (or Tamari)
*   1 tsp Sesame Oil
*   1/2 tsp Ginger, grated
*   Pinch of Red Pepper Flakes (optional, for extra spice)

**Instructions:**

1.  **Cook Rice:**  Cook rice according to package directions.  Let it sit for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it is tender.
2.  **Prepare Chicken:**  Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.  In a bowl, whisk together the cooked rice, chicken broth, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and red pepper flakes (if using).
3.  **Stir-Fry:**  Heat a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat.  Add the chicken and stir-fry until cooked through (about 5-7 minutes). Remove the chicken from the skillet and set aside.
4.  **Add Broccoli:**  Add the chopped broccoli to the wok and stir-fry until tender-crisp.
5.  **Combine and Serve:**  Add the cooked rice to the wok.  Stir-fry until the rice is heated through and the broccoli is tender-crisp.  If the broccoli is browning too quickly, add a little more soy sauce or tamari.
6.  **Serve:**  Serve the chicken and rice stir-fry immediately.

**Tips and Variations:**

*   **Spice Level:** Adjust the amount of red pepper flakes to your preference
```

## Explain the difference between a virus and a bacterium in simple terms.

```
Okay, let's break down the difference between viruses and bacteria in simple terms:

*   **Virus:** A virus is a tiny, often harmless, genetic material (DNA or RNA) that can invade and replicate inside a living host cell. Think of it as a tiny, sneaky little bug.

*   **Bacteria:** Bacteria are prokaryotic cells (cells without a nucleus and without a backbone). They are single-celled organisms that can reproduce through simple binary fission (like a normal cell dividing). They are also often found in the soil, water, and air.

In short, a virus is like a tiny, sneaky little bug that can cause illness, while a bacterium is like a single, large, harmless creature that can cause disease.
```
