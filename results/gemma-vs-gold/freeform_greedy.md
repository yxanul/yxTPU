## I have chicken, rice, and broccoli. Give me a simple dinner recipe using them.

```
Okay, here's a simple and delicious chicken and rice dinner recipe:

**Simple Chicken and Rice Stir-Fry**

This recipe is quick, easy, and packed with flavor.

**Ingredients:**

*   1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
*   1 cup cooked rice
*   1 cup chopped broccoli florets
*   1/2 cup chopped carrots
*   1/2 cup chopped onion
*   2 tablespoons soy sauce
*   1 tablespoon rice vinegar
*   1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
*   1 teaspoon sesame oil
*   1/2 teaspoon grated ginger
*   1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, for extra heat)
*   1 tablespoon cornstarch (for coating the chicken)

**Instructions:**

1.  **Prepare the Chicken:**  In a bowl, whisk together the rice, broccoli, carrots, onion, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey/maple syrup, sesame oil, and ginger.  Make sure the rice is evenly coated.
2.  **Coat the Chicken:**  Place the chicken breasts in a bowl.  Pour the chicken mixture over the rice, ensuring it's evenly coated.
3.  **Cook the Chicken:**  Heat a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat.  Add the chicken to the hot wok.  Cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until the chicken is cooked through and slightly browned.  Don't overcrowd the pan!
4.  **Add the Vegetables:**  Add the chopped broccoli and carrots to the wok.  Cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently, until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
5.  **Add the Sauce:**  Pour the soy sauce, rice vinegar, and honey/maple syrup over the vegetables.  Stir well to combine.
6.  **Season and Serve:**  Taste and adjust seasonings
```

## I'm nervous about a job interview tomorrow. Any advice?

```

*   **Prepare your answers:** Practice answering common interview questions. Don't just recite the answers; think about the context and how you can explain your skills and experience in a way that's relevant to the job.
*   **Practice your interviewing skills:** Rehearse your answers out loud, focusing on your body language and tone of voice. This will help you feel more comfortable and confident during the interview.
*   **Prepare for the questions:** Anticipate potential questions and prepare thoughtful answers. This will help you stay calm and focused during the interview.
*   **Be positive and enthusiastic:** Express your genuine interest in the company and the role. Your enthusiasm will be contagious and help to make you seem more approachable.
*   **Be professional and respectful:** Maintain a professional demeanor throughout the interview. Be punctual, polite, and respectful of the interviewer and the company.
*   **Ask thoughtful questions:** Don't be afraid to ask questions about the role, the team, and the company culture. This shows that you're engaged and interested.
*   **Dress appropriately:** Dress professionally and appropriately for the company culture.
*   **Be prepared to discuss your career goals:** Be ready to discuss your career aspirations and how they align with the company's goals.

Remember, it's okay to be nervous. Taking a few deep breaths, practicing your answers, and focusing on your strengths will help you feel more confident and prepared for the interview. Good luck with your interview
```
