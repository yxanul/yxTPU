## I have chicken, rice, and broccoli. Give me a simple dinner recipe using them.

```
Sure, here's a simple recipe for Chicken, Rice, and Broccoli:

Ingredients:
- 1 chicken breast, cut into small pieces
- 2 cups of rice
- 1 cup of broccoli florets
- 1/2 cup of chicken broth
- 1/2 cup of grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup of chopped fresh parsley
- 1/2 cup of chopped fresh basil
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large pan, heat a tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the chicken and cook until it's no longer pink in the middle, about 5-7 minutes. Remove the chicken from the pan and set it aside.
3. In the same pan, add another tablespoon of olive oil and sauté the broccoli until it's bright green and slightly crispy, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the chicken back into the pan with the broccoli and cook until it's no longer pink in the middle, about 5 minutes.
5. Add the chicken broth, Parmesan cheese, parsley, and basil to the pan. Stir well to combine.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
7. Transfer the soup to a serving dish and top it with the remaining Parmesan cheese.
8. Serve hot and enjoy!

Enjoy your simple and delicious Chicken, Rice, and Broccoli dinner!
```

## Explain the difference between a virus and a bacterium in simple terms.

```
A virus and a bacterium are both tiny living things, but they are different in several ways.

A virus is like a tiny package that contains a piece of a living cell, called a genome, and some instructions to make more copies of itself. When a virus enters your body, it tries to infect cells and use the cell's machinery to make more copies of the virus. This can cause problems like making too many copies of the virus, which can lead to sickness.

A bacterium, on the other hand, is a tiny living thing that is not a package. It is a single-celled organism that can be found in many different places, like on your skin, in the air, and in the soil. Bacteria are usually harmless to humans, but some can cause infections and diseases.

So, viruses are like tiny packages that can cause problems, while bacteria are single-celled organisms that are usually harmless.
```
